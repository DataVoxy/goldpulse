# EarTraining Product Ideas (DataVoxy)

## Concept
Web-based ear training app leveraging Tommie's conservatory/music theory degree.
Competitor: EarMaster ($5/mo), ToneDear (free), Earonman (new).

## Key Differentiators
- **Popular song references for intervals** — use familiar melodies as mnemonics
  - Minor 2nd: "Jaws" theme
  - Major 2nd: "Happy Birthday"
  - Minor 3rd: "Greensleeves"
  - Major 3rd: "Oh When The Saints"
  - Perfect 4th: "Here Comes the Bride"
  - Tritone: "The Simpsons"
  - Perfect 5th: "Star Wars"
  - Minor 6th: "The Entertainer"
  - Major 6th: "My Bonnie"
  - Minor 7th: "Somewhere" (West Side Story)
  - Major 7th: "Take On Me"
  - Octave: "Somewhere Over the Rainbow"

- **Conservatory-level progression** — structured like a real music school curriculum
- **Neuroscience-backed** — spaced repetition, optimal practice intervals
- **Web-based** — no install, works on phone/desktop
- **Free tier** — basic intervals/chords free, advanced = paid
- **Modern UI** — dark mode, gamification (XP, streaks)

## Features
- Interval recognition (melodic + harmonic)
- Chord quality identification (major, minor, dim, aug, 7ths)
- Scale identification
- Melodic dictation
- Rhythm training
- Root note indication (missing in many competitors)
- Custom exercises per key

## Monetization
- Free: basic intervals + triads
- Premium ($5/mo): all exercises, progress tracking, advanced dictation

## Tech Stack
- Web Audio API (generate tones in browser)
- Static site (Cloudflare Pages)
- No backend needed for MVP (all client-side)
